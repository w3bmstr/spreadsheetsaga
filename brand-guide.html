SpreadSheetSaga

Every sheet tells a story. Make yours clear.

The idea

A spreadsheet is a grid of cells, and a saga is a story that unfolds across them. The mark is a three by three sheet where a path of lit cells traces an S: the thread running through your data.

Clear

Numbers first. Interfaces stay quiet so results speak.

Confident

Plain verbs, no hedging. We tell you what happened and what to do.

A little storybook

One warm accent and a touch of narrative. Never cute at the cost of speed.

Logo

Typography

Two families, both open source. Space Grotesk carries the interface; IBM Plex Mono is reserved for anything that is data.

Space Grotesk

Headings, buttons, labels. Weights 500, 600 and 700. Tight tracking on large sizes (-0.02em).

IBM Plex Mono

Cell addresses, formulas, status readouts. Always tabular so columns line up.

=SUM(B2:B14) * 1.07

Voice

Talk like a sharp colleague looking over your shoulder: short sentences, active verbs, no jargon, no exclamation marks.

Say
Copied 3 x 2 cells.
That formula refers to itself. Check B7.
Nothing to undo yet.
Avoid
Oops! Something went wrong!
Circular reference error code 522.
Action unavailable at this time.
